# Online-community mining methods findings

Source: Rueger, Dolfsma, and Aalbers, “Mining and analysing online social networks: Studying the dynamics of digital peer support,” MethodsX, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mex.2023.102005

The method article presents a workflow that first mines an online community, then combines social-network analysis with text/sentiment analysis to study both relationships and content. It emphasizes longitudinal user-generated data and documents collection and analysis steps for non-specialist researchers.

Application to WizardForums: build separate interaction graphs from authorship, replies/quotes, hyperlinks, and resource sharing; combine them with content analysis rather than treating topic prevalence as social influence. Sentiment analysis should be exploratory only, because occult discourse, ritual language, sarcasm, spiritual vocabulary, and quotation can produce systematic misclassification. A mixed-method design should inspect representative threads manually and report sampling and validation.
