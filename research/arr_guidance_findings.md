# ARR guidance findings

Source: ACL Rolling Review, Reviewer Guidelines, https://aclrollingreview.org/reviewerguidelines

Key points for this research workflow:

- ARR guidance emphasizes specific, professional, neutral assessment and updating the assessment after author response and discussion.
- Reviewers should form an initial impression before searching for related work; this supports separating pre-analysis expectations from post-analysis updates.
- Submission and review information is confidential; anonymized review data may be shared for research only with consent of willing reviewers and authors.
- Ethics and reproducibility should be considered as part of review, but the paper should stand on its own.

Application to WizardForums corpus research: establish an initial analysis plan and preregistered smoke tests before inspecting topic results; keep a changelog of post-analysis decisions; conduct a structured critical review after initial results; and preserve only appropriately authorized, minimized, or de-identified content in any public artifact.
