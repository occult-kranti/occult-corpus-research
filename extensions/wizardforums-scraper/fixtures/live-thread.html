I don't recommend making pacts with celestial spirits (they're djinn, surprise!). I wouldn't know about olympic spirits though. But the spirits of the planets/stars are prisoners and tricksters at best. They want you to make pacts/contracts with them, but the terms can never be kept on your end. They're nearly impossible. Now there is a strict hierarchy, a bureaucracy, that handles the terms of the these contracts and pacts. A large part of getting an edge on them is sniffing out this hierarchy through regular and consistent conjuration. There are certain spirits that enforce the contracts, that write them, that add certain conditions, that measure and weigh the exchange being made. There are things you can do to manipulate this process. But you are quickly approaching the world of sorcery - and that is a special type of madness hunting these spirits down to manipulate the terms.
